Title: Evidence-Based Stress Management Guide
Category: Stress Management
Source: WHO & NIMH Educational Wellness Guidelines

1. Understanding Stress
Stress is the body's natural physiological and psychological response to external demands or pressures. Short-term (acute) stress can sharpen focus, whereas prolonged (chronic) stress negatively affects sleep, concentration, immunity, and mood.

2. Practical Stress Reduction Techniques
- Box Breathing (4-4-4-4 Method): Inhale slowly for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, exhale for 4 seconds, and pause for 4 seconds. This activates the parasympathetic nervous system and lowers heart rate.
- Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR): Tense a specific muscle group for 5 seconds and release for 10 seconds, moving from feet to shoulders to relieve physical tension.
- Time-Blocking and Task Prioritization: Breaking large workloads into 25-minute focused intervals (Pomodoro technique) reduces cognitive overload.
- Physical Movement: 20 to 30 minutes of brisk walking or light aerobic exercise releases endorphins and helps regulate cortisol levels.

3. Daily Preventive Habits
Maintaining consistent meal timings, limiting excess caffeine after 2 PM, and taking short screen-free breaks every hour improve daily stress resilience.
