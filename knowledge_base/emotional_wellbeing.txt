Title: Emotional Well-Being and Resilience
Category: Emotional Well-being
Source: Mental Health Foundation Educational Notes

1. Core Pillars of Emotional Well-Being
Emotional well-being refers to the ability to recognize, understand, and manage emotions constructively while adapting to life challenges.

2. Practical Habits for Emotional Balance
- Expressive Journaling: Writing down thoughts and emotions for 10 minutes daily helps process complex feelings and reduces mental clutter.
- Gratitude Reflection: Noting three specific positive events or efforts at the end of the day trains attention toward balanced perspectives.
- Healthy Boundaries: Learning to say no to non-essential commitments protects mental energy and prevents burnout.
- Social Connection: Sharing feelings with trusted friends, family members, or mentors reduces isolation and builds emotional support.

3. Building Psychological Resilience
Resilience grows through self-compassion, focusing on controllable actions rather than uncontrollable outcomes, and maintaining realistic daily routines during difficult phases.
