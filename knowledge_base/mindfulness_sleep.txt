Title: Mindfulness Practices and Healthy Sleep Hygiene
Category: Mindfulness & Sleep
Source: Sleep Foundation & Mindfulness Education Guide

1. Mindfulness Fundamentals
Mindfulness is the practice of paying intentional, non-judgmental attention to the present moment. Even 5 to 10 minutes of daily mindful breathing improves emotional regulation and reduces rumination.

2. Simple Mindfulness Exercises
- Body Scan Meditation: Slowly bring attention to physical sensations from head to toe without trying to change them.
- Mindful Walking: Focus on the sensation of footsteps, breathing rhythm, and surrounding sounds during a slow walk.

3. Sleep Hygiene Principles
- Consistent Sleep Schedule: Wake up and go to sleep at the same time every day to stabilize the circadian rhythm.
- Digital Sunset: Avoid bright screens and stimulating content 45 to 60 minutes before bedtime to support natural melatonin production.
- Sleep Environment: Keep the bedroom cool, quiet, and dark, and reserve the bed strictly for sleep and rest.
